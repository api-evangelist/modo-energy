# Prod public API Documentation

> Modo Energy's API is designed for battery operators, owners, and utilities looking to build state-of-the-art energy data systems. All the data on the Modo Energy platform is available on our API.

## API Reference
- [Modo Energy BESS Asset Database - Australia (NEM)](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/modo-energy-bess-asset-database-australia-nem.md): Lists all battery energy storage system (BESS) assets currently operating in the Australian NEM, along with key details such as rated power, energy capacity, settlement point location, and ownership.
- [ME BESS AUS NEM Breakdown](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/me-bess-aus-nem-breakdown.md): Retrieves revenue data for each battery energy storage system (BESS) asset in the Australian NEM, broken down by revenue stream.
- [AUS NEM Asset Revenue Timeseries](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/aus-nem-asset-revenue-timeseries.md): Retrieves time-series revenue data for a specific asset in the Australian NEM over a defined time range.
- [AUS NEM Asset Revenue](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/aus-nem-asset-revenue.md): Retrieves revenue data for a specific asset within the Australian National Electricity Market (NEM) over a defined time range.
- [Modo Energy Asset Database - GB](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/modo-energy-asset-database-gb.md): A database of every GB BESS asset that Modo Energy tracks.
- [Asset Operations](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/asset-operations.md): Live detailed revenue breakdown for individual GB battery assets.
- [Index Breakdown](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/index-breakdown.md): Live Leaderboard data for individual GB battery assets.
- [Monthly Leaderboard](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/monthly-leaderboard.md): GB Leaderboard results per calendar month
- [Modo Energy Asset Database - ERCOT](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/modo-energy-asset-database-ercot.md): This database contains every ERCOT BESS asset that Modo Energy tracks.
- [BESS Physical Operations and Availability](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/bess-physical-operations-and-availability.md): Availability and physical operations breakdown for individual battery assets in ERCOT. 60-day delayed data.
- [ME BESS ERCOT Breakdown](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/me-bess-ercot-breakdown.md): Revenues earned by each ERCOT battery energy storage asset broken down by individual revenue streams. 60-day delayed data.
- [Modo Energy Asset Database - NEM](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/modo-energy-asset-database-nem.md): This database contains every NEM BESS asset that Modo Energy tracks.
- [Nord Pool European Block Orders](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/nord-pool-european-block-orders.md): Details of all block orders submitted by market participants in the European Nord Pool day-ahead hourly auction. This includes the regions of Central/Western Europe (CWE) and the Nordics.
- [Nord Pool European Prices](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/nord-pool-european-prices.md): Clearing price details of all block orders in the European Nord Pool day-ahead hourly auction. This includes the regions of Central/Western Europe (CWE) and the Nordics.
- [Nord Pool European Purchase Volume](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/nord-pool-european-purchase-volume.md): Details of purchase volumes of all block orders in the European Nord Pool day-ahead hourly auction. This includes the regions of Central/Western Europe (CWE) and the Nordics.
- [Nord Pool European Sales Volume](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/nord-pool-european-sales-volume.md): Details of sales volumes of all block orders in the European Nord Pool day-ahead hourly auction. This includes the regions of Central/Western Europe (CWE) and the Nordics.
- [Nord Pool European Volume](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/nord-pool-european-volume.md): Details of all block orders submitted by market participants in the European Nord Pool day-ahead hourly auction. This includes the regions of Central/Western Europe (CWE) and the Nordics.
- [N2EX GB Block Orders](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/n2ex-gb-block-orders.md): Details of all block orders submitted by GB market participants in the N2EX day-ahead hourly auction.
- [N2EX GB Prices](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/n2ex-gb-prices.md): Clearing price details of all block orders in the GB N2EX day-ahead hourly auction.
- [N2EX GB Volumes](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/n2ex-gb-volumes.md): Details of volumes of all block orders in the GB N2EX day-ahead hourly auction.
- [Maximum Delivery Period](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/maximum-delivery-period.md): Maximum Delivery Period (MDP) is the maximum period, expressed in minutes, over which the Maximum Delivery Volume (MDV) applies. Maximum Delivery Volume is the maximum number of MWh of Offer (or Bid) that a particular BM Unit may deliver within the associated Maximum Delivery Period.
- [Maximum Delivery Volume](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/maximum-delivery-volume.md): This endpoint provides Maximum Delivery Volume data received from NGESO. Maximum Delivery Volume is the maximum number of MWh of Offer (or Bid,that a particular BM Unit may deliver within the associated Maximum Delivery Period.
- [Minimum Non-Zero Time](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/minimum-non-zero-time.md): This endpoint provides Minimum Non Zero Time data received from NGESO. The Minimum Non-Zero Time represents the minimum time that a BM Unit can operate at a non-zero level as a result of a Bid-Offer Acceptance.
- [Minimum Zero Time](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/minimum-zero-time.md): This endpoint provides Minimum Zero Time data received from NGESO. The Minimum Zero Time is the minimum time that a BM Unit which has been exporting must operate at zero or import, before returning to export; whereas if the BM Unit has been importing, the MZT indicates the minimum time that it must operate at zero or export before returning to import, if action by the System Operator (i.e. a Bid-Offer Acceptance) places it at such a level.
- [Notice to Deviate from Zero](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/notice-to-deviate-from-zero.md): This endpoint provides Notice to Deviate from Zero data received from NGESO. Notice to Deviate from Zero (NDZ) indicates the length of time required for a BM Unit to start importing or exporting energy, from a zero Physical Notification level as a result of a Bid-Offer Acceptance, expressed in minutes.
- [Notice to Deliver Bids](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/notice-to-deliver-bids.md): This endpoint provides Notice to Deliver Bids data received from NGESO. Notice to Deliver Bids (NTB) indicates the length of time between the issuing of a Bid-Offer Acceptance and the time when a BM Unit begins to deliver Bid volumes, expressed in minutes.
- [Notice to Deliver Offers](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/notice-to-deliver-offers.md): This endpoint provides Notice to Deliver Offers data received from NGESO. Notice to Deliver Offers (NTO) indicates the length of time required for a BM Unit to start delivering Offers from the time that the Bid-Offer Acceptance is issued, expressed in minutes.
- [Balancing Services Volume](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/balancing-services-volume.md): This endpoint provides Balancing Services Volume data received from NGESO. Balancing Services Volume is a volume which is received from the System Operator, which represents the volume of energy (MWh) associated with the provision of Applicable Balancing Services for each relevant BM Unit and Settlement Period. QAS can be positive or negative and is normally only provided where there is a non-zero volume.
- [Run Down Rate Export](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/run-down-rate-export.md): This endpoint provides Run Down Rate Export data received from NGESO. Run Down Rate Export (RDRE) expresses the rate of decrease in active power production (MW/minute) for a particular BM Unit which is exporting power within a particular operating range. There can be up to three of these for a given BM Unit.
- [Run Down Rate Import](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/run-down-rate-import.md): This endpoint provides Run Down Rate Import data received from NGESO. Run Down Rate Import (RDRI) expresses the rate of increase in active power consumption (MW/minute) for a particular BM Unit which is importing power within a particular operating range. There can be up to three of these for a given BM Unit.
- [Run Up Rate Export](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/run-up-rate-export.md): This endpoint provides Run Up Rate Export data received from NGESO. Run Up Rate Export (RURE) expresses the rate of increase in active power production (MW/minute) for a particular BM Unit which is exporting power within a particular operating range. There can be up to three of these for a given BM Unit.
- [Run Up Rate Import](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/run-up-rate-import.md): This endpoint provides Run Up Rate Import data received from NGESO. Run Up Rate Import (RURI) expresses the rate of decrease in active power consumption (MW/minute) for a particular BM Unit which is importing power within a particular operating range. There can be up to three of these for a given BM Unit.
- [Stable Export Limit](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/stable-export-limit.md): This endpoint provides Stable Export Limit data received from NGESO. Stable Export Limit is a positive megawatt value, expressing the minimum stable operating level at which a particular BM Unit can export power to the transmission system.
- [Stable Import Limit](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/stable-import-limit.md): This endpoint provides Stable Import Limit data received from NGESO. Stable Import Limit is a negative megawatt value, expressing the minimum stable operating level at which a particular BM Unit can import power from the transmission system.
- [Total Load Forecast per Bidding Zone - Day-Ahead (DATL)](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/total-load-forecast-per-bidding-zone-day-ahead-datl.md): Day-ahead total load forecast data for GB (the “bidding zone”), with a maximum range of 7 days in the future. Data received from Elexon Insights.
- [Wind and Solar Generation Forecast - Day-Ahead (DGWS)](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/wind-and-solar-generation-forecast-day-ahead-dgws.md): Day-ahead generation forecast for wind and solar in GB. Data received from Elexon Insights.
- [2 to 14 Days Ahead Generation availability aggregated by Fuel Type (FOU2T14D)](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/2-to-14-days-ahead-generation-availability-aggregated-by-fuel-type-fou2t14d.md): This endpoint provides a forward view of availability (also referred to as Output Usable data under the Grid Code) for generation and interconnector capacity, accounting for planned outages covering 2 days ahead to 14 days ahead; it is aggregated by Fuel Types categories.
- [2 to 156 Weeks Ahead Generation availability aggregated by Fuel Type](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/2-to-156-weeks-ahead-generation-availability-aggregated-by-fuel-type.md): This endpoint provides a forward view of availability (also referred to as Output Usable data under the Grid Code) for generation and interconnector capacity, accounting for planned outages covering availability data from 2 weeks ahead to 156 weeks ahead; it is aggregated by Fuel Types categories.
- [Day and Day-Ahead Indicated Imbalance](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/day-and-day-ahead-indicated-imbalance.md): This endpoint provides the Indicated Imbalance. Data is received by daily by midday from NGESO. Expressed as an average MW value for each Settlement period. The Indicated Imbalance forecast for each period is the difference between the sum of the PNs submitted for generation BM Units (i.e. the Indicated Generation,and the National Demand Forecast made by the System Operator.
- [Day and Day-Ahead Indicated Demand](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/day-and-day-ahead-indicated-demand.md): This endpoint provides the indicated demand forecast for the current day and day-ahead received from NGESO. The forecast is updated every half hour. The forecast for each period is the sum of all the PNs submitted for BM Units which are forecast to be importing energy. Data is presented an average MW for the Settlement Period.
- [Day and Day-Ahead Indicated Generation](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/day-and-day-ahead-indicated-generation.md): This endpoint provides the Indicated Generation received from NGESO. Data is received daily, by midday. Expressed as an average MW value for each Settlement period. The Indicated Generation forecast for each period is the sum of all the PNs submitted for BM Units which are forecast to be exporting energy, presented as a single average MW value for the Settlement Period.
- [National Day and Day-Ahead National Demand Forecast](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/national-day-and-day-ahead-national-demand-forecast.md): This endpoint provides the National Demand forecast received from NGESO. Data is available from 9am on current day and will show values for the day ahead. Expressed as an average MW value for each Settlement period. The forecast is based on historically metered generation output for Great Britain. This value INCLUDES transmission losses, but EXCLUDES Interconnector flows and demand from station transformers and pumped storage units.
- [Demand and Surplus Forecast Data (2-14 days ahead) National Demand](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/demand-and-surplus-forecast-data-2-14-days-ahead-national-demand.md): This endpoint provides the National Demand forecast received from NGESO. Data is available from 9am on current day and will show values for the 2 to 14 days ahead. Expressed as an average MW value for each Settlement period. The forecast is based on historically metered generation output for Great Britain. This value INCLUDES transmission losses, but EXCLUDES Interconnector flows and demand from station.
- [Demand and Surplus Forecast Data (2-52 weeks ahead) National Demand](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/demand-and-surplus-forecast-data-2-52-weeks-ahead-national-demand.md): This endpoint provides the National Demand forecast received from NGESO. Data is available from 4pm each Thursday and will show values for the 2 to 52 weeks ahead. Expressed as an average MW value for each week. The forecast is based on historically metered generation output for Great Britain. This value INCLUDES transmission losses, but EXCLUDES Interconnector flows and demand from station.
- [2 to 14 Days Ahead Generation availability aggregated data](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/2-to-14-days-ahead-generation-availability-aggregated-data.md): This endpoint provides a forward view of availability (also referred to as Output Usable data under the Grid Code) for generation and interconnector capacity, accounting for planned outages covering 2 days ahead to 14 days ahead. The data is aggregated at national level.
- [2 to 156 Weeks Ahead Generation availability aggregated data](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/2-to-156-weeks-ahead-generation-availability-aggregated-data.md): This endpoint provides a forward view of availability (also referred to as Output Usable data under the Grid Code) for generation and interconnector capacity, accounting for planned outages covering availability data from 2 weeks ahead to 156 weeks ahead. The data is an aggregation of all Fuel Type categories at national level.
- [Demand and Surplus Forecast Data (2-156 weeks ahead) Surplus](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/demand-and-surplus-forecast-data-2-156-weeks-ahead-surplus.md): The Demand and Surplus forecast surplus data is received hourly from NGESO. It shows the daily peak half hour MW value of Generating Plant Demand Margin for each week for the 2 to 156 weeks ahead.
- [Demand and Surplus Forecast Data (2-156 weeks ahead) Generating Plant Demand Margin](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/demand-and-surplus-forecast-data-2-156-weeks-ahead-generating-plant-demand-margin.md): The Demand and Surplus forecast surplus data is received hourly from NGESO. It shows the daily peak half hour MW value of Generating Plant Demand Margin for each week for the 2 to 156 weeks ahead.
- [Demand and Surplus Forecast Data (2-14 days ahead) Surplus](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/demand-and-surplus-forecast-data-2-14-days-ahead-surplus.md): The Demand and Surplus forecast demand margin data is received hourly from NGESO. It shows the daily peak half hour MW value of Generating Plant Demand Surplus for each day for the 2 to 14 days ahead.
- [Demand and Surplus Forecast Data (2-14 days ahead) Generating Plant Demand Margin](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/demand-and-surplus-forecast-data-2-14-days-ahead-generating-plant-demand-margin.md): The Demand and Surplus forecast demand margin data is received hourly from NGESO. It shows the daily peak half hour MW value of Generating Plant Demand Surplus for each day for the 2 to 14 days ahead.
- [Day and Day-Ahead Transmission System Demand Forecast](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/day-and-day-ahead-transmission-system-demand-forecast.md): This endpoint provides the Transmission System forecast. Data is received from 9am on current day from NGESO and will show values for the day ahead. Expressed as an average MW value for each Settlement period. The Transmission System Demand Forecast is based on historically metered generation output for Great Britain. This value INCLUDES Interconnector flows and demand from station transformers and pumped storage units.
- [Demand and Surplus Forecast Data (2-14 days ahead) Transmission System](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/demand-and-surplus-forecast-data-2-14-days-ahead-transmission-system.md): This end point provides the Transmission System forecast. Data is available from 9am on current day and will show values for the 2 to 14 days ahead. Expressed as an average MW value for each Settlement period. The Transmission System Demand Forecast is based on historically metered generation output for Great Britain. This value INCLUDES Interconnector flows and demand from station transformers and pumped storage units. Specific publish time filters may be supplied, otherwise this will retrieve the latest published forecast.
- [Demand and Surplus Forecast Data (2-52 weeks ahead) Transmission System Demand](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/demand-and-surplus-forecast-data-2-52-weeks-ahead-transmission-system-demand.md): This endpoint provides the Transmission System Demand forecast received from NGESO. Data is available from 4pm each Thursday and will show values for the 2 to 52 weeks ahead. Expressed as an average MW value for each week. The forecast is based on historically metered generation output for Great Britain. This value INCLUDES transmission losses, but EXCLUDES Interconnector flows and demand from station. This API will provide a maximum data output range of 366 days.
- [2 to 14 Days Ahead Generation availability aggregated by Balancing Mechanism Units](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/2-to-14-days-ahead-generation-availability-aggregated-by-balancing-mechanism-units.md): This endpoint provides a forward view of availability (also referred to as Output Usable data under the Grid Code) for generation and interconnector capacity, accounting for planned outages covering availability data from 2 days ahead to 14 days ahead; it is aggregated by National Grid Balancing Mechanism Units (NGC BMUs). In the context of this report, BMUs can be considered as generating units. Elexon BMUs differs from NGC BMUs by including a prefix e.g. 'T_'. The mapping between NGC and Elexon BMUs can be retrieved via reference data API endpoints.
- [2 to 156 Weeks Ahead Generation availability aggregated by Balancing Mechanism Units](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/2-to-156-weeks-ahead-generation-availability-aggregated-by-balancing-mechanism-units.md): This endpoint forward view of availability (also referred to as Output Usable data under the Grid Code) for generation and interconnector capacity, accounting for planned outages covering availability data from 2 weeks ahead to 156 weeks ahead; it is aggregated by Balancing Mechanism Units (BMUs).  In the context of this report, BMUs can be considered as generating units. Elexon BMUs differs from NGC BMUs by including a prefix e.g. 'T_'. The mapping between NGC and Elexon BMUs can be retrieved via reference data API endpoints.
- [Wind Generation Forecast](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/wind-generation-forecast.md): This endpoint provides wind generation forecast data.
- [Bid Offer Acceptance Level Flagged](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/bid-offer-acceptance-level-flagged.md): This endpoint provides bid offer acceptance data.
- [Bid Offer Data](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/bid-offer-data.md): This endpoint provides bid-offer data
- [Maximum Export Limit](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/maximum-export-limit.md): This endpoint provides Maximum Export Limit data received from NGESO.
- [Maximum Import Limit](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/maximum-import-limit.md): This endpoint provides Maximum Import Limit data received from NGESO.
- [Physical Notifications](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/physical-notifications.md): This endpoint provides Physical Notification data received from NGESO. It returns the data valid for a single settlement period. A Physical Notification is the best estimate of the level of generation or demand that a participant in the BM expects a BM Unit to export or import, respectively, in a Settlement Period.
- [Quiescent Physical Notifications](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/quiescent-physical-notifications.md): This endpoint provides Quiescent Physical Notification data received from NGESO. It returns the data valid for a single settlement period. Quiescent Physical Notifications describe the MW levels to be deducted from the Physical Notification of a BM Unit to determine a resultant operating level. The settlement period to query can be specified as a date and settlement period, or as a datetime which will resolve to the settlement period that time falls within. If a settlement period is provided, it will take precedence over the time portion of the datetime.
- [BMU IDs and Fuel Types](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/bmu-ids-and-fuel-types.md): A current list of BM units from Elexon Insights.
- [System Price and NIV (DISEBSP)](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/system-price-and-niv-disebsp.md): System price and net imbalance volume (NIV) values per settlement period calculated by Elexon.
- [Detailed System Price (ISPSTACK)](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/detailed-system-price-ispstack.md): Bid-offer stacks by settlement period, with detailed system prices generated by the most recent run of calculations by Elexon. Data received from Elexon Insights.
- [Non-BM STOR](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/non-bm-stor.md): This endpoint provides data about the Short Term Operating Reserves (STOR) that have been made use of by NGESO. This is activity that is outside of the Balancing Mechanism and takes place to meet the need to increase generation or decrease demand.
- [Output per Generation Unit (B1610)](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/output-per-generation-unit-b1610.md): Actual metered volume output in MWh for all BM units, per settlement period.  Published 5 days after delivery period.
- [Disaggregated balancing services adjustment per settlement period](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/disaggregated-balancing-services-adjustment-per-settlement-period.md): This endpoint provides disaggregated balancing services adjustment data for a single settlement period. The response includes all the buying and selling actions that occurred during that settlement period.
- [System Frequency](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/system-frequency.md): The endpoint provides data that has been received every 2 minutes the Transmission System Frequency, expressed as a hertz value for one or more spot times within that 2 minute period. The default output will be the latest published data.
- [Half-hourly generation outturn by fuel type](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/half-hourly-generation-outturn-by-fuel-type.md): This endpoint provides the half-hourly generation outturn (Generation By Fuel type) to give our users an indication of the generation outturn for Great Britain. The data is aggregated by Fuel Type category and updated at 30-minute intervals with average MW values over 30 minutes for each category.
- [Instantaneous generation outturn by fuel type](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/instantaneous-generation-outturn-by-fuel-type.md): This endpoint provides the instantaneous generation outturn (Generation By Fuel type) to give our users an indication of the generation outturn for Great Britain. The data is aggregated by Fuel Type category.
- [Initial Demand Outturn data](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/initial-demand-outturn-data.md): The endpoint provides data showing the Initial Demand outturn.
- [Initial Transmission System Outturn data](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/initial-transmission-system-outturn-data.md): The endpoint provides data showing the Transmission System Initial Demand outturn.
- [Loss of Load Probability and De-rated Margin (LOLPDRM)](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/loss-of-load-probability-and-de-rated-margin-lolpdrm.md): Loss of load probability and de-rated margin calculated by NGESO, received from Elexon Insights.
- [Day and Day-Ahead Indicated Margin](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/day-and-day-ahead-indicated-margin.md): This endpoint provides the Indicated Margin. Data is received every half an hour from NGESO. Expressed as an average MW value for each Settlement period. The Indicated Margin forecast for each Settlement Period is the difference between the sum of the MELs submitted for that period, and the National Demand Forecast made by the System Operator. The greater the value, the higher the margin between available generation capacity and forecast demand - that is to say, the more spare capacity there is forecast to be in the system.
- [Market Index Data](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/market-index-data.md): This endpoint provides Market Index Data received from NGESO. Market Index Data is a key component in the calculation of System Buy Price and System Sell Price for each Settlement Period. This data is received from each of the appointed Market Index Data Providers (MIDPs) and reflects the price of wholesale electricity in Great Britain in the short term markets. The Market Index Data which is received from each MIDP for each Settlement Period consists of a Market Index Volume and Market Index Price, representing the volume and price of trading for the relevant period in the market operated by the MIDP. The Market Price (the volume weighed average Market Index Price) is used to derive the Reverse Price (SBP or SSP).
- [Net Balancing Services Adjustment Data](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/net-balancing-services-adjustment-data.md): This endpoint provides Net Balancing Services Adjustment data received from NGESO. Net Balancing Services Adjustment data covers the buy/sell price, cost and volume adjustments for each settlement period.
- [System Warnings (SYSWARN)](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/system-warnings-syswarn.md): Systems warnings data from Elexon Insights.
- [Temperature Data](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/temperature-data.md): This endpoint provides the average degree celsius value measured at midday deemed to be representative of the temperature for Great Britain. Data is gathered from 6 weather stations. Default output will be the last 31 days. Values are received from 5pm each day.
- [Detailed BMU database](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/detailed-bmu-database.md): This dataset contains a list of BMUs which includes locational details and thorough fuel type sorting.The valid_from field begins in April 2024, as that is when this dataset started to be compiled. All new BMUs are automatically added to this list.
- [EPEX DA Half-Hourly](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/epex-da-half-hourly.md): Day-ahead half-hourly auction from EPEX. This information comes from EPEX FTP
- [EPEX DA Hourly](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/epex-da-hourly.md): Day-ahead hourly auction from EPEX. This information comes from EPEX FTP
- [EPEX Live Continuous Intraday Contract Info Report](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/epex-live-continuous-intraday-contract-info-report.md): All contracts on the EPEX Continuous Intra Day market (GB)
- [EPEX IDA2 results](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/epex-ida2-results.md): Results from the EPEX intraday half-hourly auction. There are 14 licensed Distribution Network Operators (DNOs) in Britain and each is responsible for a regional distribution services area. DNO License Areas are sometimes referred to as GSP Groups and historically have also been known as Public Electricity Supplier (PES) regions, though the latter name is no longer widely used.   The DNO License Area boundaries are unusual because they are only well defined when tested. The boundaries tend to run through rural areas and change over time whenever a new connection is added close to a boundary. For example, a new connection close to an existing boundary can approach the DNO responsible for both neighbouring License Areas to ask for a connection, and is free to choose either depending on how much they will charge - once the new connection is made the boundary is, theoretically, re-drawn to ensure that the new connection falls into the correct License Area. - [Boundaries Supply](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/boundaries-supply.md): This dataset contains approximate GIS (Geographic Information System) geospatial boundaries associated with each of the Grid Supply Points (GSPs) on the GB transmission network. The boundaries have been derived by generating Thiessen polygons around the lowest level grid asset for which we have a topological lookup back to GSP. In some cases, this means Thiessen polygons have been generated around Distribution Network Operator (DNO) assets (e.g. Bulk Supply Points, Primary Substations etc) and these Thiessen polygons have been aggregated up to GSP level using topological lookups provided by the DNOs, in other cases no DNO asset GIS data was available in time for the dataset release and the GSP boundary is a single Thiessen polygon. The GSP boundaries have been clipped to the DNO Licence Area boundaries, except where GIS data from DNOs provided evidence of a DNO serving demand in a neighbouring DNO Licence Area through bilateral agreement with the neighbour DNO. The GSP boundaries are used for regional modelling of the GB electricity transmission network, for example to aggregate distributed embedded generator energy flows onto the transmission network. This includes the regional FES analysis (https://www.nationalgrideso.com/future-energy/future-energy-scenarios/regionalisation-fes) and the Regional PV_Live outturn estimates published by The University of Sheffield (https://www.solar.sheffield.ac.uk/pvlive/regional/). There are multiple versions of the GIS boundaries, with older versions maintained here for posterity. The latest version of the GIS boundaries is 20220314. The 20181031 version of the dataset includes a lookup between GSPs and so-called Gnodes and Direct Connects. Gnodes are an entity used by teams within National Grid ESO for the purposes of modelling and monitoring the transmission network. In most cases there is a 'ONE : ONE' relationship between 'GSP : Gnode', but occasionally a Gnode can represent several GSPs ('ONE Gnode : MANY GSPs') and in some cases a Gnode represents an asset on the distribution network. In general, the relationship between GSPs and Gnodes is 'MANY : MANY'. A Direct Connect represents demand connected directly to the transmission network. They are included in this dataset because they may theoretically host embedded generation and/or may form part of a MANY:MANY relationship with a Gnode. The 20220114 version of the dataset contains only GSPs and does not include mappings between GSPs, Gnodes and Direct Connects. Some GSPs have been grouped together because it was not possible to discern which geographical regions they each serve.

- [List Config Schema Versions](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/list_config_schemas.md): Lists the simulation config schema versions currently supported for each market region: the current version and, during a release grace period, the previous (deprecated) one.  To fetch a schema document itself, call `GET /config-schemas/{market_region}/` for the latest, or `GET /config-schemas/{market_region}/{version}/` to pin a version. Use `?market_region=` to filter this list to a specific region.
- [List Forecast Runs](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/list_forecast_runs.md): Returns a paginated list of forecast runs for your company, ordered newest first by default.  Each item includes the original `simulation_config` submitted at creation. Use `GET /runs/{id}/results/{resolution}/` to retrieve results for a completed run.  Filtering: combine any of the query parameters below — they are AND'd together. `*__in` parameters accept comma-separated values. Date filters accept any ISO 8601 datetime, e.g. `2026-01-01T00:00:00Z`.
- [Create Forecast Run](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/create_forecast_run.md): Submit a new custom forecast simulation run.  Select your market region from the dropdown below to see the required and optional configuration fields for that region.
- [Get Forecast Run](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/get_forecast_run.md): Returns detail for a single forecast run.  Poll this endpoint until `status` is `ready_for_review`, then call `GET /runs/{id}/results/{resolution}/` to retrieve results.
- [Get Forecast Run Results](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/get_forecast_run_results.md): Returns results for a completed forecast run at the requested resolution.  Results are returned in column-oriented format: each entry in `columns` has a `label`, a `display_label`, and a `data` array of length `periods`. Timestamps are implicit — derive them from `data_start`, the resolution, and the array index.  Use `?columns=` to request a subset of columns by label. Omit the parameter to return all available columns.  Returns 404 if the run has not yet completed. Poll `GET /runs/{id}/` until `status` is `ready_for_review` before calling this endpoint.
- [List Custom Files](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/list_custom_files.md): Returns the custom files your company has uploaded, newest first.  Use a file's `filename` to reference it in your `simulation_config` when creating a run via `POST /runs/`. Filter with `?file_type=`.
- [Upload Custom File](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/upload_custom_file.md): Upload a custom CSV file to use in a forecast run, such as a battery degradation curve, a solar or wind generation profile, or grid charges/curtailment limits.  The file is validated on upload (CSV only, max 20 MB, and the column structure must match the selected `file_type`); invalid files are rejected with a 400 describing the problem.  **How to use the result:** take the `filename` from the response and reference it in your `simulation_config` when calling `POST /runs/`. For example, a `degradation_curve` upload goes in `simulation_config.assets.battery.degradation.profile_filename`.  Send the request as `multipart/form-data`.
- [List Nodes](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/list_nodes.md): Returns the list of valid pricing nodes for a market region.  Use the `name` field to populate the `nodes` field in your `simulation_config` when creating a nodal forecast run via `POST /runs/`.  Requires `?region=` to select the market region and `?list_type=` to select the forecast or backtest node list. Supports `?search=` to filter nodes by name.
- [Get Config Schema](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/get_config_schema.md): Returns the simulation config schema for a market region and version. `version` is a semver prefix: pass the major version (e.g. `1`, recommended) to always get the latest compatible schema in that series. Minor and patch releases never break a valid config, so a major-version pin keeps working across them.  More precise pins (`1.0`, `1.0.1`) resolve only while that version is still supported (the current schema or, during a release grace period, the previous one) — an exact pin will start returning 404 soon after any newer release, including cosmetic patch changes. Use one only to reproduce a specific past request, not in integrations.  Omit the version segment entirely (`GET /config-schemas/{market_region}/`) to get the region's current schema.
- [List Market Regions](https://developers.modoenergy.com/reference/list_market_regions.md): Returns the market regions available on the public API.  Use the `value` field to select a region when creating a run via `POST /runs/`. Config schemas at `GET /config-schemas/{market_region}/` are keyed by the GDM region name instead — list them via `GET /config-schemas/`.
